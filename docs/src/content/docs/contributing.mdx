---
title: Contributing
description: How to report bugs and suggest improvements while XN is in beta.
---

XN is in beta. Commands, settings, and the checkpoint format may still change between versions.

Issues are welcome. If something doesn't work, the docs are unclear, or you have an idea, please open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/nk4dev/xn/issues).

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

- the command you ran and its output
- the output of `xn doctor`
- your OS, and whether you train on CPU, CUDA, or MPS
